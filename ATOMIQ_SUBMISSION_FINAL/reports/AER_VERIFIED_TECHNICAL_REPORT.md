# Atomiq Hackathon — Verified Qiskit Aer Evidence

**Backend:** Qiskit AerSimulator (local simulator; **not** quantum hardware). Environment: Python 3.11.18, Qiskit 2.6.2, Qiskit Aer 0.18.2, seed 42. All three stages use the same N17+Friend Hybrid Stage 1 probabilities.

## Stage 1
- 100 unique test customers, sorted by submitted probabilities; ten highest selected. No test labels used.
- The separate Stage 1 training and benchmark methodology is in the accompanying code/report; this package provides **inference outputs**, not an independent hidden-test evaluation.

## Stage 2 — constrained selection
- Select exactly six of the top ten. Budget <=25 and capacity <=22.
- Objective: maximize sum_i (p_i * expected_deposit_value_i - contact_cost_i) x_i.
- Brute-force check: all 210 six-person subsets feasible; optimum value **43.1832040885** using unrounded Stage 1 probabilities; selected customers **C0081, C0079, C0031, C0327, C0374, C0222**; cost **14**, capacity **11**.
- QAOA: 10 qubits, Dicke weight-six initialization via Qiskit `initialize`, XY mixer, p=2, seed=42, **8,192 Aer shots**. Optimum bitstring `0101001111`, observed **236** times, best sampled objective **43.1832040885**, sampled best gap **0**; **8,192/8,192** shots feasible. Most frequent bitstring `0101101011` (593 shots) is **not** optimal.
- Statevector versus exact 210-dimensional subspace probabilities: max discrepancy **2.57e-16**. Ideal probability of exact optimum **2.8828%**. Statevector expectation of objective **33.720062**, lower than optimum.
- Circuit (including measurement but treating state preparation as a **single opaque `initialize`**): depth 12, 20 RZ, 18 RXX, 18 RYY, 10 measurements, 36 two-qubit rotations, 0 swaps. **This depth is not a hardware-native decomposed depth.** `initialize` prepares a 1024-amplitude state and may require many native gates; a device-specific transpilation and layout is necessary before any real-hardware resource claim.

## Stage 3 — campaign outcome probability
- q0..q5 correspond to C0081, C0079, C0031, C0327, C0374, C0222. Prepare each with Ry(2 asin sqrt(p_i)), then measure; count bitstrings with at least three ones.
- Exact independent-Bernoulli probability **0.67795147187**.
- Ideal Aer: **5515/8192 = 0.6732177734**, absolute error **0.0047336984**, approximately 0.473 percentage points. Wilson-style shot interval is in stage3_aer_evidence.json.
- Illustrative noisy Aer: **5392/8192 = 0.658203125**, with depolarizing error 0.001 and asymmetric readout errors 0->1: 0.02 and 1->0: 0.03. **This model is not device calibrated.**
- Circuit: 6 qubits, 6 Ry gates, 6 measurements, depth 2, no two-qubit gates; all unchanged in reported local transpilation.

## Reproducibility, caveats, and interpretation
- All measurement counts in `aer_evidence/*.csv` are **genuine simulator samples provided by the user**, not generated by hardware.
- Stage 2 measured-best gap zero does **not** imply the optimization converged with certainty or has a quantum speedup. All 210 classical candidates were checked, so no speedup claim is justified.
- Noise illustration is synthetic. Stage 1 predictive probabilities are estimates, so the Stage 3 binomial shot confidence interval excludes predictive-model uncertainty and potential cross-customer dependence.
- For hardware submission, compile Dicke state preparation and mixer to the target device's native gates, report its full transpiled depth, connectivity, calibration data, physical qubits, job IDs and hardware readout mitigation separately.
